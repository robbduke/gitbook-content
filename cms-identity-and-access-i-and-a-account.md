# CMS Identity & Access (I\&A) Account

A CMS Identity & Access (I\&A) account is a critical requirement for any group or individual provider enrolling with Medicare. Setting up this account is a key step in the credentialing process and ensures you can manage your Medicare enrollment efficiently and securely.

### What Is an I\&A Account? <a href="#undefined" id="undefined"></a>

The **CMS Identity & Access (I\&A) Management System** is an online portal managed by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). It is used to:

* Create and manage your Medicare enrollment records in PECOS (Provider Enrollment, Chain, and Ownership System)
* Approve surrogacy (delegated access) for third parties—such as credentialing services—to manage your enrollments on your behalf
* Maintain secure access to your Medicare provider information

**Every group and individual provider billing Medicare must have an I\&A account.**

### Why Is an I\&A Account Important for Onboarding? <a href="#undefined" id="undefined"></a>

Without an I\&A account:

* You cannot be added as a surrogate, meaning your credentialing partner cannot act on your behalf
* You cannot manage, enroll, or revalidate your Medicare enrollment online
* Credentialing and enrollment will be delayed until your account is set up and surrogate access is approved

**Timely creation of your I\&A account is essential for a smooth onboarding and credentialing process.**

### What Information Do You Need to Create an I\&A Account? <a href="#undefined" id="undefined"></a>

To register for an I\&A account, you will need:

* Your National Provider Identifier (NPI) or your organization’s NPI
* Your Social Security Number (SSN) or Employer Identification Number (EIN/TIN)
* Your contact information (email, phone, address)

### How to Create Your I\&A Account <a href="#undefined" id="undefined"></a>

Follow these steps to set up your account:

1. **Go to the CMS I\&A System:**\
   Visit the official CMS I\&A Management System website.
2. **Click “Create Account”:**\
   Select the option to create a new account and follow the on-screen instructions.
3. **Enter Required Information:**\
   Provide your NPI, SSN or EIN/TIN, and contact details as prompted.
4. **Complete Registration:**\
   Finish the registration process and verify your email address.
5. **Approve Surrogate Access:**\
   Once your account is active, log in and approve your credentialing partner (such as \[Your Company Name]) as your surrogate. This allows them to manage your Medicare enrollment on your behalf.

**Tip:** Creating an I\&A account is free and typically takes about 15 minutes.

### What If You Don’t Have an I\&A Account? <a href="#undefined" id="undefined"></a>

If you are unsure whether you have an I\&A account, or know you do not have one, you should:

* Confirm your status during onboarding (e.g., “Do you currently have a CMS I\&A account for your practice or individual provider profile?”)
* If you answer “No” or “Unsure,” follow the steps above to create your account as soon as possible

Credentialing cannot proceed until your I\&A account is active and surrogate access is granted.
