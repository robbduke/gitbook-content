# Group Insurance Requirements

Proof of insurance is required for group credentialing. Insurance payers, hospitals, and state agencies need to verify that your organization is protected against both clinical and non-clinical risks. This page provides an overview of the types of insurance you’ll need and links to detailed instructions for each.

### What this is <a href="#what-this-is" id="what-this-is"></a>

Group insurance requirements refer to the documentation your practice or facility must provide to show you have adequate insurance coverage. The two most commonly required types are **general liability insurance** and **professional liability (malpractice) insurance**. Some payers or states may require additional coverage, such as workers’ compensation or cyber liability insurance.

### Why we need it <a href="#why-we-need-it" id="why-we-need-it"></a>

Insurance documentation is required to:

* Protect patients, staff, and the public from financial losses due to accidents or errors
* Meet payer, hospital, and state regulatory requirements
* Ensure claims and contracts are processed without delay

### Types of Insurance Required <a href="#types-of-insurance-required" id="types-of-insurance-required"></a>

* General Liability Insurance
* Professional Liability (Malpractice) Insurance
* Other Insurance Types (if applicable)

For each insurance type, you’ll typically need to submit:

* Certificate of Insurance (COI)
* Policy number
* Coverage limits
* Expiration date
