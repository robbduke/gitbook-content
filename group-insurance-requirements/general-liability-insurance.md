# General Liability Insurance

## General Liability Insurance <a href="#general-liability-insurance" id="general-liability-insurance"></a>

General liability insurance protects your organization from non-clinical risks and is a standard requirement for group credentialing.

### What this is <a href="#what-this-is" id="what-this-is"></a>

General liability insurance covers injuries to patients, visitors, or others on your premises (such as slip-and-fall incidents), property damage, and related legal costs.

### Why we need it <a href="#why-we-need-it" id="why-we-need-it"></a>

Most payers, hospitals, and states require general liability insurance to:

* Protect your organization from everyday business risks
* Ensure your practice meets basic business insurance standards
* Reduce risk for all parties involved

### What to collect <a href="#what-to-collect" id="what-to-collect"></a>

Submit the following:

* Certificate of Insurance (COI)
* Policy number
* Coverage limits
* Expiration date

### Common issues <a href="#common-issues" id="common-issues"></a>

* Submitting an expired or incomplete COI
* Coverage limits below payer or state minimums
* The name on your insurance certificate must match your legal business name and Tax ID

### Don’t have general liability insurance? <a href="#dont-have-general-liability-insurance" id="dont-have-general-liability-insurance"></a>

Contact your insurance broker or carrier to obtain coverage and request a current COI before submitting credentialing paperwork.
