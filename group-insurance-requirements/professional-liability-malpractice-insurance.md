# Professional Liability (Malpractice) Insurance

Professional liability (malpractice) insurance is essential for protecting your group against claims related to patient care.

### What this is <a href="#what-this-is" id="what-this-is"></a>

Professional liability insurance covers claims related to errors, omissions, or negligence in patient care. It is the core insurance for healthcare providers and organizations.

### Why we need it <a href="#why-we-need-it" id="why-we-need-it"></a>

Required by most payers, hospitals, and state regulations to:

* Protect your organization against malpractice claims
* Meet credentialing and contracting requirements
* Ensure compliance with legal standards

### What to collect <a href="#what-to-collect" id="what-to-collect"></a>

Submit the following:

* Certificate of Insurance (COI)
* Policy number
* Coverage limits
* Expiration date

### Common issues <a href="#common-issues" id="common-issues"></a>

* Missing or expired COI
* Coverage limits below required minimums
* Mismatched legal names or Tax IDs on documentation

### Don’t have professional liability insurance? <a href="#dont-have-professional-liability-insurance" id="dont-have-professional-liability-insurance"></a>

Contact your insurance broker or carrier to secure coverage and request a current COI before applying for credentialing.
