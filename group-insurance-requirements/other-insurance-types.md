# Other Insurance Types

Some payers or state laws may require additional insurance coverage for your group practice.

### What this is <a href="#what-this-is" id="what-this-is"></a>

Other insurance types may be required based on your services, staff, or payer contracts. Common examples include:

* **Workers’ Compensation Insurance**\
  Required if your group employs staff. Covers work-related injuries or illnesses.
* **Cyber Liability Insurance**\
  Increasingly requested to protect against data breaches and cyber threats.
* **Employment Practices Liability, Directors & Officers, Errors & Omissions**\
  Sometimes requested for larger or multi-specialty groups.

### Why we need it <a href="#why-we-need-it" id="why-we-need-it"></a>

These policies may be required to:

* Meet state employment laws
* Protect against emerging risks (like cyberattacks)
* Satisfy specific payer or facility contracts

### What to collect <a href="#what-to-collect" id="what-to-collect"></a>

For each policy, submit:

* Certificate of Insurance (COI)
* Policy number
* Coverage limits
* Expiration date

### Unsure if you need additional coverage? <a href="#unsure-if-you-need-additional-coverage" id="unsure-if-you-need-additional-coverage"></a>

Check your payer contracts, state requirements, or consult your insurance broker for guidance.
